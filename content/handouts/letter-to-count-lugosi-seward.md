# A Letter to Count Lugosi

<div class="letter-header">Count Lugosi,</div>

I am looking forward to my visit with you. It has been some time, and I am eager to meet Elizabeth and to learn she is with child. I am sorry to hear you are not well. I hope my services can be helpful to Elizabeth and yourself.

<div class="signature">Your friend,<br>Doctor John Seward</div>
