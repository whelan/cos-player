# The Count's Journal

<div class="book-spread">

<div class="book-page">

Elizabeth has shown no signs of change. I feed her as often as I can, but each day she slowly devolves. Unless I can lift this curse, I fear she is lost.

<div class="whisper">
I can only assume it was the Burgomaster of the Village of Barovia that betrayed me to Lord Strahd.
</div>

The Monster does not respond to my commands. I must keep it in the darkness, chained, to keep it calm. I must try again.

</div>

<div class="book-page">

<div class="corruption">
One of the zombies has ventured onto the estate. I have captured it and chained it in the pit. My cannibals have toyed with it but know not to attempt to bite it. Fascinating, the living and dead. Both feed on the flesh of the living.
</div>

My cannibals are cursed and run afoul, yet they may serve a purpose. A dozen more, and I will release them upon the village of Barovia, who will pay for their betrayal.

The door to my stair bears the four signs and no lock. It opens to me from below at dusk and seals at my back. Only when the rite is walked above does it open again, and so each night I kneel for her before I go down.

The rite must be walked the way the ceiling walks it — the star given the first place, and the dark given the last word. I have knelt beneath that painted sky so many nights that I no longer need to look up.

</div>

</div>

<div class="book-page">

*A loose sheet, folded into the back of the journal. Newer ink.*

Igor still asks after Krezk. I promised him the bells and the wall and his own people, once she was well again. He believes it. He is the only one in this house who still believes anything I say.

</div>

</div>
