# Unsent Letters to Lord Strahd

*A bundle of letters tied with a string, every one folded and none of them sealed. Three lie on top. The hand begins wide and careful and grows smaller with each sheet.*

<div class="letter-header">My Lord Strahd,</div>

For twenty years I have carried your letters to Vallaki and to Krezk, and never once have I broken a seal. I beg you to weigh that against a single season of foolishness. Whatever was done beneath this house is ended. The rooms are bricked up and the books are burned.

I ask only for an audience, and that my wife and my son be spared the consequence of my error.

<div class="signature">Your faithful courier, still,<br>Lugosi</div>

---

<div class="letter-header">My Lord,</div>

<div class="corruption">
Boris does not wake by day. Elizabeth will not eat what I bring her, and then she eats it.

I have read everything. I have sent to Vallaki for more. There is a rite in these books, and I believe what was bound can be loosed from the same side it was bound. Tell me only whether I am right.

I will drive your carriage again. I will drive it anywhere. I will bring you whatever the road brings me.
</div>

<div class="signature">L.</div>

---

<div class="whisper">
My Lord,

My Lord,

She asked for you today. She did not say my name. I told her you were coming.
</div>
