# Whispers Beneath the Amber

<div class="official-record">
An account of a journey into the eastern heights, and of the return.<br>
Printed from a manuscript whose author did not sign it.
</div>

*The printed text of this copy is crowded on every page with notes in a second hand. They are reproduced where they stand.*

<div class="book-spread">

<div class="book-page">

<div class="official-record">
I went up because a man told me the road existed. I will not write down where he said it, and I will not write down what I paid him, because both of those things would put you on it.
</div>

<div class="whisper">
Bought at Vallaki, off the old man at Walpole's. A poor writer and an honest one — two qualities that rarely travel together.
</div>

<div class="official-record">
Four of us went up. The pass took two. I set this down plainly and early so that no reader can say afterwards that the book made it sound easy.
</div>

<div class="whisper">
He means to frighten. It reads as grief.
</div>

<div class="official-record">
The hall was cold in a way that had nothing to do with the mountain. The walls were not stone. There were shapes held in them, and I looked once and did not look again, and I have been asked a hundred times since what the shapes were, and a hundred times I have said: I looked once.
</div>

<div class="whisper">
Eleven times I have read this page. It is the eleventh reading that is the trouble, and not the page.
</div>

</div>

<div class="book-page">

<div class="official-record">
Nothing spoke to us. I want that written plainly, because it is the thing I am always asked and the thing nobody believes. <strong>Nothing spoke.</strong>

And yet each of the two of us who came down came down with a sentence in his head that he had not thought of himself. Mine I have never repeated. His he repeated often, and then he stopped, and then he was not a man I knew any more.
</div>

<div class="whisper">
A sentence you did not think of. Yes. Yes — that is exactly the size of it.
</div>

<div class="official-record">
I took nothing out of that hall. Let me be exact, because exactness is all I have left to give: I took nothing out <em>that I chose</em>. A piece of the wall no bigger than a thumbnail was in my coat when I reached the lower road. I do not remember stooping.
</div>

<div class="whisper">
Nor do I.
</div>

<div class="whisper">
It is warm. Amber does not hold warmth. I have tested it against the stove, against the snow, against my own palm in the dark, and it is warm in all three, and in the third it is warmest.
</div>

</div>

<div class="book-spread">

<div class="book-page">

<div class="official-record">
I have been asked whether it offers anything. It does not offer. It does not ask. It does not bargain, threaten, tempt or plead. I have set that sentence down in four different books now and each time some reader writes to me convinced I am protecting it.
</div>

<div class="corruption">
It has never once asked me for anything. Eight months. Not one thing asked. That is what I cannot get past — a creditor who will not name the debt, and so the debt cannot be paid, and so it only sits.
</div>

<div class="official-record">
My advice, for whatever a survivor's advice is worth, is this: do not test whether you can put it down. The test proves nothing, because you will pass it the first ninety times.
</div>

<div class="corruption">
Put it in the desk. Did that. Put it in the strongbox in the cellar. Did that, and was down there again before the candle had burned a finger.<br><br>
If I put it in the ground I will dig it up. Better to keep it where I can see it. That is not weakness, it is arithmetic.
</div>

</div>

<div class="book-page">

<div class="official-record">
The last thing, and then I am done with this and with all of it. The others went up wanting power. I went up wanting to have gone up. I am not better than they were. I am only luckier, and I do not expect the luck to be permanent.
</div>

<div class="corruption">
E. asked why the lamp is lit at this hour. Told her the ledgers. She did not believe me and did not press, which is worse.
</div>

<div class="corruption">
Not tonight. Not tonight. Not to
</div>

<div class="blood">
<strong>It does not want anything. I am the one who wants.</strong>
</div>
